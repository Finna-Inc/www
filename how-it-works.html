The first conversation

It starts with a conversation.

Your office takes shape from the first conversation. Finna starts with the questions a family office would ask, and your answers become your family’s first picture.

  1. Who and what matters to your family?
  2. What do you own, owe, protect and manage?
  3. Who currently advises you?
  4. What important knowledge or responsibility sits with one person?
  5. What would you like to grow, protect or carry forward?

Safe by design

Your money stays where it is.

  • Finna never holds your assets.

    Your money stays with the banks and providers you already use.

  • Read-only, always.

    Anything you connect can be read, never moved. Finna cannot move money.

  • Everything exports.

    Every line your household entered, at any time, on every plan.

  • Private by default.

    Your principals decide who sees what. Nothing leaves the household without that decision.

What it costs

Free for your own household.

The five desks and Learn cost nothing for your own household. You pay only when your office reaches further: more people, legal structures, outside parties or specialist work.

Finna charges for scope. Never for information, and never for understanding.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I need documents to start?

No. Start with what you know, and add statements and documents later. Nothing is required to see your first picture.

Does Finna give financial advice?

No. Finna is a technology platform that helps your household organize and understand its finances. The decisions stay with you, and with the specialists you choose.

Can Finna move my money?

No. Finna never holds your assets, and anything you connect is read-only.

Who can see our information?

Your household’s principals decide. Nothing is shared outside the household without that decision.

Can we leave and take everything with us?

Yes. Everything your household entered exports at any time, on every plan.
