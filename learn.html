The Map

One map, and it grows with you.

The tutorial is the trunk. Each desk is a branch, and one branch is about you. Every quest you answer is a leaf, and a branch shows what you have done, the path open now and one step ahead. Never more, so nothing is teased.

A quest

One question at a time.

A quest takes about a minute, and it is about your own household, not a textbook family.

  1. A question about your record.

    One question on your own numbers, with a hint if you want it and the reason behind the answer. Getting it wrong costs nothing.

  2. Every answer is a leaf.

    A right answer adds 40 XP and a leaf on the branch, and See it in your office takes you straight to the page the quest is about.

  3. A path ends in a badge.

    Answer every quest on a path and its badge is yours, and the next step on the branch opens. Some paths end in a certificate instead.

A branch about you

Your Financial Identity.

Before a family writes its rules, it helps to know itself. This branch asks the questions every family office starts with, and explains what each answer means.

  • Who is the money for? Yourselves first, your children, or the generations after.
  • How long could it wait? The years before most of it is needed.
  • What would you do in a bad year? Sell some to stop the fall, hold and wait it out, or add while it is cheaper.

You learn and answer here, in Learn. Your answers are kept on the Policy desk, where your family’s rules are built on them, and you answer again when life changes.

Inside Policy

Badges and certificates

A badge for a path. A certificate for knowing it.

A badge records a path completed. A certificate records understanding shown in a test, and it is what opens more of the platform.

Five certificates at launch

Investment Foundation, Public markets, Core portfolio, Private markets and Private equity, each at the end of its own path.

A fair test

Eight questions drawn from fourteen, with no hints and no Freya. Six right to pass, and every retake is free.

What a certificate opens

Investment Foundation, for one, opens currency exposure on What you hold. What opens depends on what you have learned, not on what you own.

A certificate opens parts of Finna. It is not a professional qualification, and it does not make anyone an accredited investor or change the rules that apply to them.
