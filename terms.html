The terms we both keep.

The short version: your record is yours, Finna never holds or moves your money, and nothing in Finna is advice. These terms set out the rest, for this website and for your family office.

Who we are

By using the website or opening an office, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use either.

What Finna is, and is not

Finna is a technology platform that helps you organize and understand your household’s finances. Finna Inc. is not registered with or regulated by any financial authority. We do not give investment, tax, legal or insurance advice, and nothing in Finna is a recommendation, or an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security or investment.

Decisions about your money are yours. For advice on your own situation, talk to a qualified professional.

Your account

You must be 18 or older to open an office, and the office is for your own household. Give us true details, keep your sign-in to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used it. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

On the free office two adults share one household record, and both can see and change everything in it. When you invite someone in, such as a family member or an adviser, you choose what they can see, and that choice is yours.

Your record is yours

Everything your household enters, uploads or connects stays yours. You allow us to store it, process it and show it to you and to the people you let in, and to let Freya work from it, so that we can run your office. We never sell it.

You can export everything your household entered at any time, on every plan, including after a change of plan. When you add information or documents about other people, such as your children, your parents or a trust, make sure you have the right to.

How we handle personal information is set out in the privacy notice.

Freya

Freya is Finna’s AI assistant. She prepares summaries and answers questions from your record, and her work appears in your access log. Like any AI, she can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, so check anything that matters before you act on it. What she says is information, not advice.

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of Freya’s work, as shown on the Pricing page.

Figures and what-if tools

The figures in your office are only as good as what is entered and connected, and values change. Check them before you rely on them.

The what-if tools show what might happen under stated assumptions. Their projections are hypothetical in nature, do not reflect actual investment results and are not guarantees of future results. Results may vary with each use and over time. Each tool explains how its numbers work.

Connections and your money

Connections to your accounts are read-only. Finna cannot move money and never holds your assets.

The banks, custodians and other providers you connect run their own services under their own terms. We are not responsible for those services, or for the accuracy of what they send us.

Learn

Learn’s paths, badges and certificates record what you have learned inside Finna. They are not professional qualifications or licenses. Some parts of the office open as you complete paths. That is about understanding the tools, never a judgment of whether an investment suits you.

Plans and payment

Family Office is free for your own household, and no card is needed. Family Office Plus costs $15 a month per household, in US dollars, with any tax that applies. It is billed monthly in advance and renews each month until you cancel.

You can cancel at any time in the app. Cancelling stops the next payment, Plus stays open until the end of the month you have paid for, and we do not refund part of a month unless the law requires it. When a paid plan ends, you drop back to the free office: anything the paid plan created becomes read-only, never deleted, and all of it still exports.

If a price changes, we tell you before it applies, and you can cancel before you are charged the new price. Family Office Pro is not open yet. Joining its waitlist commits you to nothing.
