You’ve been sold the investing desk.

You’ve never been offered the office.

Your bank or adviser looks after your investments. That is one desk. A family office runs four more: Records, Policy, Protection and Succession. For most families, nobody runs those at all.

Finna gives you all five desks.

The platform

Five desks, one office.

Every family office runs the same five desks. Finna runs them from one household record, and each desk tells you, in plain words, what needs you today.
