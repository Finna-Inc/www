What we hold, and why.

The short version: this site keeps almost nothing about you. The waitlist keeps your name and email so we can invite you in when your place opens.

Last updated 27 September 2026

Who we are

Finna Inc., a company in the United States, runs this website and the waitlist and decides how the information described here is used. Questions go to privacy@finnafamilyoffice.com.

What the website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising tools, and stores nothing in your browser. Its fonts load from our own files, not from a third party.

The service that hosts the site keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, your browser and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.

What the waitlist collects

Your first name, last name and email address, and the date you joined. We use them for one thing: to invite you in when your place opens.

Joining is your consent to that, and you can withdraw it at any time by leaving the list.

Who handles it

Mailchimp, a service run by Intuit in the United States, stores the waitlist and sends our emails for us. It may record whether an email was delivered and opened. It handles your details only to provide that service to us.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

Where it is held

In the United States. If you join from elsewhere, your details are transferred there.

How long we keep it

Until you leave the list or ask us to delete it. If the waitlist closes, we delete it.

Your choices

Every email we send has a link to leave the list. You can also ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to privacy@finnafamilyoffice.com. We answer within one month.

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK or California, the law gives you further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

The office itself

When the Finna platform opens, it will hold far more than this site does. It will come with its own privacy notice, which you will see before you add anything.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the new version appears here with a new date. If a change affects how we use your waitlist details, we will tell you by email first.

Questions about your information?

Write to privacy@finnafamilyoffice.com and we will answer within one month.
