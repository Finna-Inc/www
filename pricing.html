On every plan

What never changes.

  • Understanding is never for sale.

    Learn is the same on every plan, and what you learn opens the same things whichever plan you are on.

  • Your record is always yours.

    No plan changes what you can see in your own record, and Finna never holds or moves your money.

Questions

About the plans.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. The five desks, Learn and your whole record cost nothing for your own household, and no card is needed. You pay only when your office reaches further.

What does Plus add?

The rest of your family, every generation, with the full Family Charter. Two advisers you invite, with access you set desk by desk. And a quarterly family report.

Can I bring my parents in?

Yes. On the free office two adults share the record, and parents, children and siblings are recorded as people without a login. Plus gives every generation its own login and the Family Charter, so your parents can add what they hold and the family can agree who decides what. How it works

When does Pro open?

In the next few months. Join the Pro waitlist to hear first, and start with Family Office or Plus today: everything you set up carries straight into Pro.

What happens if we stop paying?

You drop back to the free office. Anything a paid plan created becomes read-only, never deleted, and all of it exports.

Does paying change what we can invest in?

No. No plan changes what Learn opens or what you can see in your own record. Finna is a technology platform and gives no investment advice on any plan.

Are prices in USD?

Yes. Prices are per household, per month, in US dollars.
