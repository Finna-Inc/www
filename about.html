Why Finna exists.

An office of your own — the complete picture, the patient strategies, the quiet attention, the continuity.

For a hundred years, one kind of family has had an office.

For a hundred years, families with great wealth have had something better than products: an office of their own. People who saw everything, coordinated everything, and answered only to the family. Everyone else got accounts, apps and advice that never quite joined up.

We built Finna because that difference always seemed wrong — and because, for the first time, it doesn’t have to exist. Technology can now carry what once took a staff of ten: the complete picture, the patient strategies, the quiet attention, the continuity. So we built your own family office — much more than investing.

— Christian Lindekilde, Founder

Who we are.

Operators who have each done a piece of this before.

Christian Lindekilde

Christian holds a PhD in finance and law, on M&A. He was an executive in UBS’s Global Family Office, and has spent his career advising and building family offices across more than USD 30bn of family wealth. He later raised over USD 280m of capital for US technology companies. He started Finna on one conviction: that having an office of your own should depend on understanding your money, not on the size of it.

Carolin Feindor

At LGT Capital Partners, Carolin ran the operations behind USD 60bn in private markets, across 30 private equity boards in Luxembourg and Liechtenstein. Before that, PwC in Zurich and New York. She is a chartered accountant (FCCA), a Swiss-licensed audit expert and a Liechtenstein CPA.

Chris Mills

Chris came to finance from AI, cybersecurity and games. He builds the intelligence behind Freya, the security model that lets a household put everything in one place, and the engineering that keeps it calm.
