Our story

For more than a century, one kind of family has had an office.

Families with great wealth have long had something better than products: an office of their own.

People who saw everything, coordinated everything and answered only to the family.

Everyone else got accounts, apps and advice that never quite joined up.

We built Finna because that difference always seemed wrong, and because for the first time it doesn’t have to exist. Technology can now carry much of what once took a staff of ten: the complete picture, the quiet attention, the continuity. That is why we built your own family office, and made it much more than investing.

— Christian Lindekilde, Founder

The team

Who we are.

Operators who have each done a piece of this before.

CEO

Christian Lindekilde, PhD

Christian holds a PhD in finance and law, with a thesis on M&A. He was an executive in UBS’s Global Family Office, and has spent his career advising and building family offices across more than $30bn of family wealth. He later raised over $280m of capital for US technology companies. He started Finna on one conviction: that having an office of your own should depend on understanding your money, not on the size of it.

COO

Carolin Feindor

At LGT Capital Partners, Carolin ran the operations behind $60bn in private markets, across 30 private equity boards in Luxembourg and Liechtenstein. Before that, PwC in Zurich and New York. She is a chartered accountant (FCCA), a Swiss-licensed audit expert and a Liechtenstein CPA.

CTO

Chris Mills

Chris brings more than 25 years of experience in technology, AI and security. He builds the intelligence behind Freya, the security model that lets a household put everything in one place, and the engineering that keeps it running quietly.
