Security

Security and your data.

Your household’s record is the most private thing Finna holds. This is how it is protected, who can see it, and how you take it with you.

The short version

Four things that are always true.

  • Read-only connections.

    Anything you connect can be read, never moved. Finna cannot move money, and never holds your assets.

  • Two-factor sign-in.

    A second step when you sign in, a recovery contact, and a list of every device signed in to your office.

  • A log you cannot edit.

    Who looked at or changed what, and when. Entries are added, never edited.

  • Everything exports.

    Every line your household entered, in one file, on every plan, including after a downgrade.

Inside the app

You can see who has seen what.

Profile › Security shows how you sign in, every device signed in to your office, and the access log.

The log names every look and every change: the person, what they did and when. Freya’s work is in it too, so when she prepares your weekly briefing or reads a document, the entry says so.

Who sees what

Your household decides.

Your household

On the free office two adults share the record, and both see and edit everything in it. Children, parents and siblings are recorded as people, without a login.

The wider family

On Plus every generation can have its own login, and the Family Charter records who decides what. Bringing your parents in

Your advisers

Invite your attorney, CPA or banker, with access limited to what they cover. Until you invite them, advisers are listed in the record, not let into it.

Freya

Finna’s assistant works from your record to prepare your briefing and answer your questions, and every piece of her work appears in the access log.

Your data stays yours

Take it with you, any time.

Export everything in one file: the people, every valuation with its history, the details of your documents, your policy versions, your cover, the will record and the access log.

Changing plan deletes nothing. Anything a paid plan created becomes read-only and still exports. How this website handles the little it collects is on the Privacy page.
