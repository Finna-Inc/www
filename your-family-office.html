Your family office

Five desks, one office.

A family office keeps the whole picture and never stops paying attention. Finna gives your family one of its own: five desks around one household record, with Freya, Learn and the Family Vault beside them.

Explore your family office See how it works

The five desks

What each desk does.

Every desk reads the same household record, so nothing is entered twice. Open one to see it on a screen.

01 · Gather it

Records

One statement for the whole household: every account, property, loan, policy and document, and the people involved. The other four desks all work from it.

What stays yours. You choose what goes in, and who in the family sees it.

Inside Records

02 · Govern it

Governance

Your family’s rules for its money, written down in plain words: how much to keep in each kind of investment and how much cash to keep at hand. Finna checks what you own against them every day and tells you when something slips outside.

What stays yours. Finna supplies the template and never suggests a number.

Inside Governance

03 · Grow it

Investment

Everything you hold elsewhere, in one view and measured against your own policy: brokerage, pensions, bonds and private holdings, with every fee added up.

What stays yours. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Inside Investment

04 · Protect it

Protection

Every policy the household holds, reviewed in one place: what is covered, what is missing and what needs attention, with renewals before they lapse.

What stays yours. What to do about each gap is your decision, with the specialist you choose.

Inside Protection

05 · Pass it on

Succession

The people, the papers and the reasons, kept together: wills, trustees and beneficiaries, and the knowledge behind them, so the next generation inherits the understanding too.

What stays yours. The household decides who sees what, and when the next generation is invited in.

Inside Succession

Around the desks

Everything else in the office.

The desks do the work. These four keep it together.

  • Home and the Timeline

    Your first picture, then one card at a time. The Timeline shows what is coming: renewals, reviews and the dates that matter.

  • The Family Vault

    Every document filed where it belongs, from the will to the policies to the statements, and found by the people who need it.

  • Learn

    Short lessons in plain language, built around your own numbers. What you learn opens what comes next.

  • Freya

    Your AI chief of staff. She reads everything, does the arithmetic and brings you one briefing a week.

Why it works

Built on the same five principles.

Money’s real problem is coordination. For more than a century, the wealthiest families have solved it with an office of their own, built on five principles: one team on your side of the table, the complete perspective, a memory that doesn’t fade, discretion and continuity.

What is a family office?
