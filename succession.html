What it does

Wealth, knowledge and responsibility, prepared together.

Papers in order

Wills, beneficiaries, ownership and instructions: organized, current and findable by the people who will need them.

Knowledge that doesn’t live in one head

The reasons behind decisions, the people to call, the way things work: written down and shared, so nothing important depends on one person remembering.

The next generation, invited in

Children and heirs join the office as family members, not as a project. They see what is right for their age, learn at their own pace and grow into responsibility years before they need it.

Wealth becomes family before it becomes legacy.

Your partner may see money differently. Your children will inherit more than assets. The decisions you make today can shape your family for decades.

Finna holds each person’s view, and the household’s.

Bring your parents in

One office for every generation.

Succession works best when the people it concerns are part of it. In Finna the family joins in three steps, at the pace the conversation allows.

  1. FreeStart with your household.

    Two adults share the office. Parents, children and siblings go into the family tree as people, without a login: entered once, and read by every desk.

  2. PlusInvite them in.

    Every generation can have its own login, so your parents can add what they hold and where the papers are, and work from the same record as you.

  3. PlusAgree who decides what.

    The Family Charter records who decides what, when the family meets and what was decided. Invite your attorney or CPA, with access limited to what they cover.

Continuity without ceremony.

No family summit required. Carrying wealth forward becomes something your office quietly maintains: a living picture, not a binder on a shelf.
